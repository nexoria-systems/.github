## Nexoria Systems

Nexoria is a European work platform for company knowledge, enterprise search, AI, office applications and connected workflows. It combines its own knowledge store and software with connections to existing business systems.

### Find knowledge and put it to work

- **Nex AI and enterprise search:** find information in permitted company documents and review answers through source references.
- **Nexoria Drive:** organise files in your own knowledge store and access them as a drive in Windows Explorer.
- **Doc, Calc and Slides:** work with documents, spreadsheets and presentations.
- **DocStudio:** create new content from company knowledge.
- **Workspaces and permissions:** organise shared knowledge and team access.
- **OCR and audio transcription:** make different content formats usable.
- **Tasks, actions and automations:** connect knowledge with the next work step.
- **Chatbots:** make knowledge available on websites and in customer service.
- **Optional Mail, Meet and VoiceBot:** add communication workflows.

### Connect existing systems

Connections to SharePoint, OneDrive, Google Drive and other knowledge sources bring external information together with Nexoria's own storage. Integrations and actions in CRM, ERP and other applications are configured for each organisation's systems and permissions. Teams can find knowledge across sources and continue working in Nexoria's own applications.

### Where it runs

Files and the knowledge index are hosted in Germany. Nex uses Mistral AI for answer generation in France. Current product capabilities, integration scope and security documentation are available on the website.
