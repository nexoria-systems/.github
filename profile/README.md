## Nexoria Systems

Nexoria is a European work platform for mid-sized companies. It combines
cloud storage, indexed company knowledge and the AI assistant Nex with the
office applications Doc, Calc and Slides.

Nex answers questions about a company's own documents and cites the source
for each answer. Workspace permissions also apply to the AI: content of a
workspace is not available in search or in AI answers to people who are not
members of it.

### What it includes

- **Nex**, the assistant over the company's own documents
- **Knowledge search** across stored files, without consuming AI tokens
- **Doc, Calc and Slides**, built on LibreOffice, working with `.docx`,
  `.xlsx` and `.pptx`
- **Nexoria Drive**, storage mapped as a drive in Windows Explorer, with
  versioning and restore
- **Nexoria Mail**, mailboxes over IMAP and SMTP, with reply suggestions,
  summaries, translation and a phishing check
- **Nexoria Meet** for audio, video and group calls
- **Chatbots** for websites and a **VoiceBot** for answering calls

Available as a Windows and Linux application. Browser-based use is in
development.

### Where it runs

Dedicated servers in German data centres, no public cloud. The language
model is Mistral, based in Paris.
